BENCHMAN XT - PHYSICAL I/O LIST (from benchman.hal)
====================================================
Pin numbers are the HAL names (din-0 = first input on the module).
"1st EL1008" = EL1008-digital-in-1, "1st EL2008" = EL2008-digital-out-1.

INPUTS - 1st EL1008 (8-ch digital input)
----------------------------------------
 Reed switches on the ATC air cylinders (the "-limit-switch" nets):
 din-0      atc-down-limit-switch   reed switch, ATC cylinder down position
 din-1      atc-up-limit-switch     reed switch, ATC cylinder up position
 din-2      atc-in-limit-switch     reed switch, ATC cylinder in position
 din-3      atc-out-limit-switch    reed switch, ATC cylinder out position

 Front panel buttons (the "-frontpanel-button" nets, read inverted):
 din-4      din-4-not   platter-up-down-frontpanel-button
                       -> toggles the ATC down solenoid (dout-1). Lowering is only
                          allowed when the machine is on and no program is running
                          (idle or paused is fine); raising is always allowed.
 din-5      din-5-not   platter-index-frontpanel-button
 din-6      din-6-not   drawbar-release-frontpanel-button

 din-7      not used

 (Only din-4 has logic so far; din-5 and din-6 are not used by any logic yet.)

OUTPUTS - 1st EL2008 (8-ch digital output)
------------------------------------------
 dout-0     spindle-air-solenoid     Spindle bearing air supply solenoid
 dout-1     atc-down-solenoid        ATC down solenoid        (driven by din-4 toggle OR a future automatic TC request)
 dout-2     atc-in-solenoid          ATC in solenoid          (nothing drives it in HAL yet)
 dout-3     tool-length-sensor-blast Tool length sensor air blast (nothing drives it in HAL yet)
 dout-4     spindle-clamp-solenoid   Spindle clamp solenoid   (nothing drives it in HAL yet)
 dout-5     taper-blast-solenoid     Taper blast solenoid     (nothing drives it in HAL yet)
 dout-6     mist-coolant-solenoid    Mist coolant solenoid    (driven by iocontrol.0.coolant-mist, M7)
 dout-7     flood-coolant-pump-relay Flood coolant pump relay (driven by iocontrol.0.coolant-flood, M8)

 While the platter is manually down (toggle state only, not an automatic TC),
 HAL sets motion.feed-inhibit and motion.jog-inhibit.

NOT USED IN HAL
---------------
 2nd EL1008 (EL1008-digital-in-2), 2nd EL2008 (EL2008-digital-out-2),
 EL3202-analog-in-1, atc-servo.

SERVO DRIVES (A6-EC) - CN1 HD15 connector wiring
------------------------------------------------
 Not defined in benchman.hal (the drives handle these signals internally).
 Wiring below is from the user, with the CN1 signal names from the A6-EC
 manual (Electrical Installation, CN1 user control terminal). Same
 premade HD15 cable on each drive (X, Y, Z).

 CN1 pin  cable color   A6 signal            connected to
 -------  ------------  -------------------  ---------------------------------------
   10     Blue          DI1 (pos limit)      positive limit switch
    9     Green         DI2 (neg limit)      negative limit switch
   14     Pink          COM-                 common side of both limit switches
   13     White         COM+  (DI common)    jumpered to pin 15
   15     Light Green   +24V (internal)      jumpered to pin 13
    5     Red           DO3+  (Brake)        negative side of servo brake relay coil
    4     Brown/White   DO3-                 tied to 24V ground
   (relay coil other side goes to +24V)
    8     Yellow        DI3 (home switch)    not used
    7     Orange        DI4 (probe 2)        not used
   11     Purple        DI5 (probe 1)        not used
    1     Black         DO1+ (servo ready)   not used
    6     Red/White     DO1- (servo ready)   not used
    3     Brown         DO2+ (fault)         not used
    2     Black/White   DO2- (fault)         not used
   12     --            (not in the cable color list)

 Per drive, in HAL terms:
   positive limit switch -> CN1 pin 10 (DI1) -> lcec.0.<x|y|z>-servo.pos-limit (not netted)
   negative limit switch -> CN1 pin 9  (DI2) -> lcec.0.<x|y|z>-servo.neg-limit (not netted)
   brake relay           <- CN1 pins 5/4 (DO3, the drive's "Brake" output)

 Check against the DI/DO function parameters in the drive: DI1 = P-OT,
 DI2 = N-OT, DO3 = brake (BRK-OFF) are the factory defaults. The 60FD
 pins in ethercat-conf.xml expose the limit inputs but nothing in HAL uses them.
